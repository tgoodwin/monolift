## Critique of claude's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** yes — The path accurately follows the chain from `main` through the CLI and server setup to the OAuth2 callback handler.
- **Minimal:** yes — All 7 steps represent distinct layers of the application activation.

### Edge types
| Step | claude's label | Verdict | Suggested label (if different) | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | `struct-literal-field-assignment` | RENAME | `direct-function-call` | The step is from `StartWebServer` to the `newRouter` function. While the result is assigned to a field, the activation of `newRouter` itself is a simple static function call. |
| 5 | `http-handler-registration` | RENAME | `direct-function-call` | The step is from `newRouter` to `ui.Serve`. `ui.Serve` is called directly to obtain a handler; the registration (`appMux.Handle`) happens after or as part of this call, but the jump to `ui.Serve` is a direct call. |
| 6 | `method-value-handler-registration` | OK | — | Good specific label for `handler.oauth2Callback`. |

### Other notes
Claude's line citations are generally accurate, though `ui.Serve` starts at `ui.go:16` rather than `17`.

## Critique of codex's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** partially — The citation for `oauth2Callback` in Step 6 is `oauth2_callback.go:15`, but the function actually starts at line 19.
- **Minimal:** no — Steps 7 through 11 are side-branches or prerequisite setup logic (manager construction) called within `oauth2Callback`. They do not lie on the direct activation path from `main` to `Profile`. In a minimal trace, `oauth2Callback` should jump directly to `Profile` (or the `Provider` interface).

### Edge types
| Step | codex's label | Verdict | Suggested label (if different) | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | `configuration-gated-direct-call` | OK | `direct-function-call` | "Configuration-gated" is useful context but the mechanism is a direct call. |
| 4 | `struct-literal-handler-field-assignment` | RENAME | `direct-function-call` | Same reasoning as for Claude; the jump to `newRouter` is a direct call during initialization. |
| 7-11 | Various | REJECT | — | These steps should be collapsed or moved to resolution notes as they are not part of the primary activation chain. |
| 12 | `map-backed-interface-method-dispatch` | OK | `interface-method-dispatch` | Accurately describes the complexity of resolving the `authProvider` variable. |

### Other notes
Codex provides excellent detail on *how* the static analyzer resolves the interface (Steps 9-11), but including them as sequential steps in the "activation path" is misleading as it implies a deeper call stack than actually exists for the `Profile` call.

## Cross-trace observations

The traces converge on the 7-step core path: `main` -> `cli` -> `daemon` -> `server` -> `ui` -> `oauth2_callback` -> `google.Profile`. Claude and gemini correctly identify this as the minimal path. Codex's 12-step trace is more of a "resolution trace" than an "activation path," including the constructor branch for the provider implementations which, while necessary for a compiler to resolve the target, isn't part of the direct execution sequence leading to the root.
