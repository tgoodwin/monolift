### Summary

- **Path length:** 6 steps
- **Edge types encountered:** `direct-function-call`, `conditional-direct-function-call`, `goroutine-launch-of-closure`
- **Hardest edge:** Step 3, because a static analyzer must include the body of an anonymous function launched as a goroutine and recognize that its loop body is activated by a local channel carrying work items.
- **Static-analysis feasibility:** The full path is statically resolvable with ordinary Go call-graph construction, control-flow reachability, goroutine-function-literal handling, and local channel flow; there is no reflect/plugin/runtime function-pointer boundary on this path.
