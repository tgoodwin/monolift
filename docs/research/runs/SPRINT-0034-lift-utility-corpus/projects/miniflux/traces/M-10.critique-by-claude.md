## Cross-trace observations

Both peer traces take the **UI submit** entrypath (`POST /subscribe` → `submitSubscription`); my trace took the **REST API** entrypath (`POST /v1/discover` → `discoverSubscriptionsHandler`). Steps 1–4 are identical across all three traces; the divergence is at Step 5 (which sub-handler the root router routes into). All three paths are 7 steps and share the same edge-type shape: `direct-function-call ×5 + http-handler-registration ×1 + method-call-on-concrete-type ×1`. Gemini's edge-type labels are the closest to the canonical minimal shape; codex over-specializes Steps 3, 4, 5 with bespoke labels that conflate control-flow guards or downstream side effects with the resolution mechanism of the edge itself, while gemini's main defect is a target-line error in Step 1, not a labeling one.
