### Other notes
- The `conditional-direct-function-call` label appears four times. It conflates two orthogonal axes (callee-resolution mechanism vs. path-condition tracking), which is a granularity bug for the schema we're building. If the schema needs to express conditional reachability, that belongs as a separate per-edge attribute, not as a new edge type.
- Step 3's resolution note correctly observes that the channel controls *which* values flow but not callee identity — this is exactly the right way to describe the goroutine-launch boundary, and it's the kind of explicit dispatch-vs-data separation the rest of the trace would benefit from.
