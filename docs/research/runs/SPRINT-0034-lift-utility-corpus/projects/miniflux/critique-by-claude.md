## Overlooked

The union of the three Phase 1 drafts (claude, codex, gemini) covers the substantive lift surface of the miniflux binary: the full refresh pipeline (RefreshFeed/ProcessFeedEntries/ParseFeed), the per-page CPU/IO regions (ScrapeWebsite/ExtractContent/SanitizeHTML/ProcessEntryWebPage), the favicon work (UpdateOrCreateFeedIcon), the integration fan-outs (PushEntries/SendEntry), the discovery and bulk-import paths (FindSubscriptions/OPML Import), and the read-side rewriter (mediaproxy). The remaining candidates I would consider — `oauth2.{googleProvider,oidcProvider}.Profile` at `internal/oauth2/google.go:57` and `internal/oauth2/oidc.go:64` — are present in my own draft (C-11) and so are not "missed by all three drafts." I do not have a region to add here.

## Overall observations

Codex and gemini converge cleanly on the obvious top set — RefreshFeed, ProcessFeedEntries, SanitizeHTML, ScrapeWebsite/ExtractContent, ParseFeed, PushEntries, UpdateOrCreateFeedIcon — and on which regions are marginal (icon work, OPML import, media-proxy rewrite). They diverge in two directions: codex includes the user-pull entrypoint `ProcessEntryWebPage` and the integration `SendEntry` (both correct picks the gemini draft omits), while gemini reaches for `CreateFeed` and `EstimateReadingTime` (both weaker — `CreateFeed` is structurally redundant with its own constituent picks, and `EstimateReadingTime` fails the compute-envelope criterion). The systematic difference is **citation rigor**: every line cite I spot-checked in codex's draft was correct or off by one, while gemini's draft is internally well-reasoned but its file:line cites are wrong almost everywhere — both region roots (e.g. SanitizeHTML at `:193` vs actual `:217`) and caller sites — to a degree that suggests the cites were generated without grounding in the source tree.
