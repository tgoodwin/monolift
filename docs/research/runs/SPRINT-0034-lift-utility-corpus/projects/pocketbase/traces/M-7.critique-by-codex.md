- **Minimal:** no — The email-change route is a good shortest-route choice, but Steps 2-3 and 7-8 can be collapsed into command-callback and HTTP-handler callback edges. Add explicit mail-client construction/config and SMTP send-hook finalizer edges.

- **Correct:** partially — The password-reset route is real, but several citations are drifted: `main`/`Start` are `examples/base/main.go:19` and `:119`, `apis.Serve` is `apis/serve.go:61`, `NewRouter` is `apis/base.go:19`, `bindRecordAuthApi` is `apis/record_auth.go:10`, and `recordRequestPasswordReset` is `apis/record_auth_password_reset_request.go:16`. ### Other notes
The `evaluation/pocketbase/` prefix is acceptable if used consistently, but the line numbers should still be verified against that tree. The hardest edge is not just `e.Mailer.Send`; it is the combination of `core.App.NewMailClient` interface dispatch, settings-guarded implementation selection, mailer hook finalizer dispatch, and SMTP send-hook continuation.

## Cross-trace observations

Both foreign traces correctly converge on the route-to-mail-helper-to-`mailer.Mailer.Send` shape, and both identify SMTP as selected by `BaseApp.NewMailClient` when SMTP is enabled. They diverge mainly in route choice: claude's email-change route is closer to the canonical minimal path, while gemini's password-reset route adds an avoidable async edge. Both should replace the final direct SMTP fallback with the hook-finalizer path through `SMTPClient.Send`.
