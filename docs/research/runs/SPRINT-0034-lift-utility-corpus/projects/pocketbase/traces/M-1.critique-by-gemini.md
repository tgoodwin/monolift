## Cross-trace observations

- **Convergence:** All three traces (including gemini's own) agree on the fundamental path: `main` → `app.Start` → `Serve` → `NewRouter` → `api.download` → `api.createThumb` → `fsys.CreateThumb`.
- **Divergence:** Claude and Codex are much more rigorous than Gemini in documenting the HTTP request dispatch (the "gap" between route registration and handler execution). Claude's use of `method-value-as-callback` and documenting the `Action` field flow is the most useful for compiler design.
- **Canonical Path:** Claude's trace is the closest to canonical, as it captures the subtle but important closure-capture and field-flow mechanisms that bridge the framework layers.
