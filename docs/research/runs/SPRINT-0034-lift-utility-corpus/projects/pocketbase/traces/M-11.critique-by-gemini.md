## Cross-trace observations

The traces converge on the initial CLI/API bootstrap (`main` → `Start` → `Serve` → `NewRouter`). They diverge significantly in the "middle-tier" dispatch: Claude maps the production hook system (`OnRecordRequestPasswordResetRequest`), Codex maps a flatter utility path, and Gemini (me) provided a slightly abbreviated version of the production path. Claude's trace is the most faithful to the actual complexity of the PocketBase architecture and provides the best resolution for compiler-readiness assessment.
