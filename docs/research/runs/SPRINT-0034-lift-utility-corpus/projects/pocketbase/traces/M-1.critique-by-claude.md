## Critique of codex's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** yes — every file:line citation I spot-checked (`pocketbase.go:166/169/200`, `cmd/serve.go:24/39`, `apis/serve.go:61/72/217`, `apis/base.go:19/46`, `apis/file.go:26/45/84/171/209/216/225`, `tools/router/router.go:130/143`, `tools/router/group.go:118/136`, `tools/filesystem/filesystem.go:489`) lands on the claimed code.
- **Minimal:** mostly — Step 8 ("Serve hook builds and installs the HTTP handler") is *not* on the activation path to `CreateThumb`. The `OnServe` Trigger callback installs `e.Server.Handler = handler`, but the same `handler` is what `mux.HandleFunc` (Step 9) registers actions on. Step 8 is a sibling sub-graph that builds the mux that Step 9's analysis already covers; a minimal trace can drop it. Otherwise the 11 remaining steps match the canonical path.

## Critique of gemini's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** partially — file citations are systematically off by 5–10 lines and several are wrong:
  - `examples/base/main.go:102` is `migratecmd.MustRegister`, not `app.Start()` (which is at line 119).
  - `pocketbase.go:176` is inside `Execute`'s doc comment; `Start` is at line 166.
  - `cmd/serve.go:15` is `var allowedOrigins`; `NewServeCommand` declared at line 14, body opens at 19.
  - `apis/serve.go:56` is a doc comment; `Serve` is at line 61.
  - `apis/serve.go:67` is `app.RunAllMigrations()`; `NewRouter` called at line 72.
  - `apis/base.go:31` is `pbRouter.Bind(panicRecover())`; `NewRouter` declared at line 19.
  - `apis/base.go:49` is `bindHealthApi`; `bindFileApi` is at line 46.
  - `apis/file.go:34` is inside `bindFileApi` body; the function declaration is at line 26.
  - `apis/file.go:54` is a struct-field doc comment; `sub.GET("/{collection}/...", api.download)` is at line 45.

  Step 2 also lists From and To both as `cmd/serve.go:25`, a self-edge that does not represent any call relation. Steps 7/8 (`apis/file.go:171/209/225`) and the region root are correctly cited.
- **Minimal:** no — but minimality is not the issue; the trace is *over-collapsed*. It elides three non-trivial dispatch points that any compiler-style analysis must explicitly resolve:
  1. Cobra `Execute` → `RunE` dispatch (no step covers `pb.RootCmd.Execute()` reaching the `serve` `RunE` value).
  2. HTTP mux dispatch — there is no step for `tools/router/router.go:130` (the `mux.HandleFunc` registration) nor for the `Hook.Trigger` chain (`tools/hook/hook.go:153–173`) that connects `Route.Action` to `api.download`. Gemini collapses Step 6 ("Register download handler") and Step 7 ("Call internal thumbnail generation helper") with no intermediate dispatch step, implying `api.download` is called directly from `bindFileApi` registration — which is wrong.
  3. The `singleflight.DoChan` callback — Step 8 jumps straight from `apis/file.go:225` to `tools/filesystem/filesystem.go:489`, but `apis/file.go:225` is *inside the closure body* passed to `DoChan` at line 216. Gemini's trace skips the closure-capture edge entirely.

### Other notes

- The summary's edge-type list is a strict subset of what the path truly contains, because the omitted steps are precisely where the non-`direct-function-call` edges live.

## Cross-trace observations

The three traces converge on the canonical path *shape*: `main → Start → RunE closure → apis.Serve → NewRouter → bindFileApi → registration of api.download → HTTP dispatch → download → createThumb → DoChan closure → fsys.CreateThumb`. Codex and I agree on 11 of 12 steps and disagree mainly on edge-type *naming conventions* (codex uses framework-flavored compound labels; I prefer atomic mechanism labels). Gemini diverges meaningfully: it skips three non-trivial dispatch points (cobra `Execute`, the router/hook chain, and the `singleflight` closure body) and has systematic file:line drift suggesting it was working from a stale or imagined snapshot of the source. Closest to the canonical minimal path: codex (with Step 8 dropped) and my own trace are roughly tied; gemini's is too compressed and too misquoted to be load-bearing as written.
