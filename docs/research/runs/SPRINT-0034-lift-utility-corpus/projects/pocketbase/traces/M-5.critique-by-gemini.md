## Cross-trace observations

The traces for Claude and Codex converge on the `settingsTestEmail` path, which is shorter and avoids the `app.OnRecordRequestPasswordResetRequest().Trigger` hook and `routine.FireAndForget` goroutine launch found in gemini's trace. Claude and Codex provide much better granularity for the Cobra command dispatch, breaking it down into struct-field assignment and subsequent invocation. Codex's `value-conditioned-direct-call` is the most precise label for the final jump into the mailer. Claude's trace is slightly more comprehensive in its resolution notes regarding how function values flow through the router. Both Claude and Codex are superior to gemini's original trace in terms of path minimality and edge-type granularity.
