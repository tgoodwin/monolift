## Critique of codex's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** yes — The path is factually correct and exhaustive.
- **Minimal:** no — At 22 steps, this trace is far from minimal. Steps 4, 5, 6, and 10 are `direct-function-call` edges within the same package or to simple helpers (`Load` -> `changeConfig` -> `unsyncedDecodeAndRun` -> `run`) that a compiler resolves trivially. These should be collapsed into a single "Apply Configuration" hop.

## Cross-trace observations

The traces converge on the critical execution path: `Main` -> `cmdRun` -> `caddy.Load` -> `App.Start` -> `ServeHTTP` -> `executeTemplate`. They all correctly identify the middleware dispatch at `routes.go:337` as the hardest edge to resolve statically due to the registry-based instantiation and closure wrapping. Claude's trace is the closest to a "canonical minimal path" because it focuses on the cross-module hops and non-trivial interface/closure boundaries without getting bogged down in Caddy's internal configuration-flow boilerplate.
