## Overall observations

Codex and gemini converge cleanly on the heavy file paths (extraction, image post-processing) and both make the same mistake on `SearchPostsForUser` — a marginal pick where the compute is in the search engine and the path is user-synchronous; the merged set should drop it. Where they meaningfully diverge, codex is the more rigorous reader: its candidate anchors land on real worker entry points (`BulkIndexPosts`, `sendFileToRemote`, `BulkImport`) that match the rubric's "already async, naturally batched" shape, whereas gemini repeatedly anchors at sub-regions or mischaracterized helpers (`generateThumbnailImage` instead of `postprocessImage`, `GetMessageForNotification` framed as template rendering when it is i18n placeholder selection). Gemini's strongest contribution is `PBKDF2.Hash` — the only one of the three drafts to surface it, and a clean fit for the rubric. Both critiqued drafts are systematically weaker than my own on criterion 5 (latency tolerance): they admit synchronous request-path picks that should have been excluded.
