## Critique of codex's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** yes — every cited file:line resolves and the call chain holds end-to-end. Verified `commands/root.go:15-17`, `commands/server.go:30,34-37,39,57,60,73,95,104`, `api4/api.go:185,344`, `api4/file.go:33-34,77,114,133,183`, `api4/handlers.go:50-53`, `web/handlers.go:145,355`, `app/file.go:774,840,846,931`.
- **Minimal:** no — three steps add no resolution work. **Step 4 (`app.NewServer`)** is data-flow setup for the argument passed to `api4.Init`; it is not on the call path to `postprocessImage`. **Step 8 (`server.Start`)** and **Step 9 (HTTP dispatch via `http.Server`)** can collapse into a single "HTTP handler registration → invocation" edge — splitting them inflates the path with `http.Server.Serve` plumbing that is identical for every HTTP handler in the binary and is not part of resolving *this* callback. The minimal shape is "register at `file.go:34`, invoke at `web/handlers.go:355`" (one edge), which would bring the path from 13 to 10 — close to gemini and one above mine.

## Critique of gemini's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** yes — citations check out (`main.go:20`, `root.go:15,17`, `server.go:36,39,57,60,95`, `api/api.go:185,344`, `file.go:33-34,77,114,133,183`, `app/file.go:774,846,931`).
- **Minimal:** yes — 10 steps; collapses route registration and handler invocation into Step 6, which matches the canonical minimal shape. One could argue Step 6 elides the `web.Handler.HandleFunc` field write performed by `APISessionRequired`, which is the actual function-value-in-struct-field edge — but treating "the router invokes the registered handler" as a single black box at the HTTP-registration abstraction level is defensible and is what a route-aware analyzer would summarize.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | `callback-registration` | RENAME | `function-value-in-struct-field` (registered via `init-function`) | "Callback" is fine vernacular but is too generic; the language-level mechanism is "function value stored in a struct field, later read back and invoked." `callback-registration` is also what gemini uses for HTTP routes (Step 6) — using the same label for two structurally similar but distinct mechanisms (Cobra `RunE` vs. mux router) loses information. |

### Other notes
- The trace is the most concise of the three but loses some resolution detail: the `web.Handler.HandleFunc` field-write/read mechanism is mentioned only as "decorator that eventually invokes the handler." A schema that needs to enumerate edge mechanisms will want that detail surfaced.
- Static-analysis feasibility note ("Mattermost… avoids interfaces… concrete struct pointers") is accurate for this path.

## Cross-trace observations

All three traces converge on the same sequence of waypoints (`commands.Run` → Cobra `RunE` → `runServer` → `api4.Init` → `InitFile` → handler register → `uploadFileStream` → `uploadFileSimple` → `(*App).UploadFileX` → `(*UploadFileTask).postprocessImage`) and agree on edge mechanism for the body of the path. They diverge on **HTTP-edge granularity**: gemini fuses register+invoke into one edge (10 steps), I fuse them but mention the two underlying mechanisms (9 steps, since I omit `app.NewServer`), codex splits into four (13 steps) by walking through `server.Start` and `http.Server.Serve`. They also diverge on the **Cobra `RunE` label** — codex's `init-time-function-field-dispatch`, gemini's `callback-registration`, and my `function-value-in-struct-field` are three different abstraction levels for the same edge. Gemini's path is closest to the canonical minimal shape; mine is one shorter but at the cost of some terseness around HTTP dispatch; codex's is correct but inflated by setup-shaped steps that don't add resolution complexity.
