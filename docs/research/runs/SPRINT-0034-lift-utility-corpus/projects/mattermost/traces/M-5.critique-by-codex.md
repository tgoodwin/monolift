## Critique of gemini's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** partially — The broad path is real, but several citations drift: `main` is at `main.go:19` and the `commands.Run` call is at `main.go:20`; `RootCmd.Execute()` is at `root.go:17`; the `AddCommand` registrations are at `export.go:54-57`, not `export.go:68-71`.
- **Minimal:** no — Steps 2 and 3 should be collapsed into one registered-callback dispatch edge. Step 3 also describes the `RunE` assignment as if it were itself an execution edge, skipping the command-tree registration/dispatch relation.

### Edge types
| Step | gemini's label | Verdict | Suggested label (if different) | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | `method-call-on-concrete-type` | COLLAPSE | — | The concrete method call to Cobra is true but unnecessary in the minimal static path; the compiler-facing edge is from `RootCmd.Execute()` to the reachable registered handler. |
| 3 | `function-value-in-struct-field` | RENAME | `registered-struct-field-callback` | This captures only `RunE: bulkExportCmdF`; the real non-trivial edge requires modeling `BulkExportCmd` registration under `ExportCmd` and `RootCmd`, then Cobra's callback invocation. |

### Other notes
Avoid making `Cobra` itself an edge-type concept. It should be represented as a general registered function-valued struct-field callback pattern with library dispatch.

## Cross-trace observations

Both traces converge on the same CLI path and correctly identify the final `a.BulkExport(...)` call as a concrete `*app.App` method call. They diverge mainly in how they model the Cobra boundary: Claude gives the right supporting facts and is closest to canonical after collapsing Steps 2/3, while Gemini has citation drift and treats the `RunE` assignment too much like the activation edge itself.
