## Critique of claude's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** partially - The main call chain and most citations are real, but the trace compresses worker construction/registration and the watcher channel sender into Step 7/8 notes rather than path edges. It also misstates the `RunEssentialJobs` assignment: `app/server.go:513` is the guard; the field is set in `app/options.go:77-78`.
- **Minimal:** mostly yes - `JobServer.StartWorkers` -> `Workers.Start` is a trivial wrapper and can be collapsed. If the trace is meant to model activation rather than only call graph reachability, the watcher `PollAndNotify` -> channel send edge should be added, not hidden in Step 8 notes.

## Critique of gemini's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** partially - The broad path is recognizable, but many citations are wrong in the checked source: `main.go:20`, `root.go:17`, `server.go:57/60/73`, `jobs/server.go:79`, `jobs_watcher.go:81`, and `elasticsearch/indexing_job.go:25` are the relevant lines. It also skips the side-effect import/init registration, `common.NewIndexerWorker`, `RegisterJobType`/`AddWorker`, and the watcher send path.
- **Minimal:** no - The trace is under-specified rather than merely short. `StartWorkers` can be collapsed as a wrapper, but worker construction, registration into the worker map, and the inter-goroutine channel handoff must be represented or explicitly named as collapsed subedges.

## Cross-trace observations

Both traces converge on the same downstream worker loop and internal calls: `IndexerWorker.Run` -> `DoJob` -> `IndexBatch` -> `IndexPostsBatch` -> `BulkIndexPosts`. They diverge mainly on whether worker registration, package-init callback wiring, and the watcher channel handoff are first-class path edges. Claude's trace is closest to the canonical minimal path, but it should split or relabel its overloaded Step 7/8; Gemini's needs line-citation repair and several missing edges added.
