- **Path length:** 9 steps.
- **Edge types encountered:** `direct-function-call`, `function-value-in-struct-field` (registered via `init-function`), `method-call-on-concrete-type`, `http-handler-registration`.
- **Hardest edge:** Step 2 (Cobra `RunE` dispatch). The call from `RootCmd.Execute()` to `serverCmdF` is mediated by Cobra's runtime traversal of its command tree and a function-value field assigned in a separate package's `init()`.
