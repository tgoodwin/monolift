### Summary

- **Path length:** 13 steps.
- **Edge types encountered:** `direct-function-call`, `function-value-in-struct-field` (Cobra's `RunE` and `SimpleWorker.execute`), `method-call-on-concrete-type`, `closure-capture`, `struct-literal-field-assignment`, `interface-upcast-into-container`, `interface-method-dispatch` (over `map[string]model.Worker` and again over `AppIface`), `goroutine-launch`, `channel-send-receive`.
- **Hardest edge:** Step 11 — resolving the call through `SimpleWorker.execute`. The analyser must connect a closure constructed in `recap.MakeWorker` (Step 7), passed three arguments deep through `NewSimpleWorker`, deposited in a struct field, and invoked from a different package via a field-load on a value that is itself reached through a `map[string]model.Worker` interface dispatch (Step 9). Doing this precisely requires field-sensitive points-to analysis plus closure tracking; doing it monomorphically depends on enumerating every call site of `jobs.NewSimpleWorker` to know what closures can land in `execute`.
- **Static-analysis feasibility:** Feasible end-to-end. Every edge is statically resolvable in principle: the Cobra `RunE` field is set at package-init time, the `model.Worker` map has a single producer (`RegisterJobType`), and `AppIface` has exactly one production implementer (`*App`). The two goroutine/channel hops (`go w.Run()` and the watcher → worker.jobs send) are reachability edges only — they do not need value-flow analysis. There is no `reflect`/plugin/runtime dynamic dispatch on this path. An alternative, much shorter path exists in tests (`server/channels/app/recap_test.go:272` calls `th.App.ProcessRecapChannel(...)` directly via `method-call-on-concrete-type`), but that is not part of the production binary's activation path.
