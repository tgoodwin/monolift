## Overall observations

Both foreign drafts converge cleanly with my draft on file extraction and image processing, and Claude also converges on outgoing webhooks and bulk export. Claude is generally more rigorous about async/background activation, though it overstates the link-preview path and keeps a per-post indexing candidate despite a state-independence failure. Gemini is useful for surfacing pure CPU/helper ideas, but it systematically overvalues thin helpers and synchronous auth/admin orchestration relative to the compute-envelope and latency/failure criteria.
