## Critique of codex's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** partially — The path is verifiable, but Step 3 and 4 unnecessarily step into the middleware wrapper factory (`Perm`) rather than jumping from the registration site to the handler target.
- **Minimal:** no — Steps 3 and 4 should be collapsed into a single `http-handler-registration` edge. By stepping into `Perm` (the factory) and then into the handler from the wrapper's `next(c)` call, the trace adds a step that describes the middleware execution flow rather than the minimal static link between the entrypoint and the region root.

### Edge types
| Step | codex's label | Verdict | Suggested label (if different) | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | `http-handler-registration-with-wrapper` | RENAME | `http-handler-registration` | The step actually points to the `Perm` factory signature, which is a `direct-function-call` in the context of registration. |
| 4 | `closure-captured-function-value` | COLLAPSE | — | This step represents the runtime invocation of the captured handler. In a minimal static path, the registration site (Step 3) is the point of interest. |

### Other notes
Codex uses absolute-ish paths (`evaluation/listmonk/...`) which is slightly more verbose than necessary but accurate. It correctly identifies the closure capture mechanism at the middleware boundary, which is the primary static analysis challenge for this region.

## Cross-trace observations

The traces converge on the primary bootstrap path (`main` -> `initHTTPServer` -> `initHTTPHandlers`) and the final resolution of `m.Render` on a concrete type. They meaningfully diverge on the treatment of the `auth.Perm` middleware: Claude (and Gemini) consolidate the registration into a single logical step, while Codex decomposes it into factory registration and closure invocation. Claude's trace is the most concise and follows the canonical minimal path most closely.
