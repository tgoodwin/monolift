## Critique of gemini's trace

- **Correct:** partially - It identifies the same real worker/map/interface shape, but several citations are stale or wrong: `main()` is `cmd/main.go:193`; messenger append is `:213`; `go mgr.Run()` is `:262`; `postback.New` starts at `postback.go:69`; worker dispatch is `manager.go:523`, not `:526`.

Claude and Gemini use the campaign dispatch at `manager.go:523`, while my trace used the tx `msgQ` dispatch at `manager.go:554`; both share the same map/interface resolution.
