### Edge types
| Step | codex's label | Verdict | Suggested label (if different) | Reasoning |

| 1 | `goroutine-launch-on-concrete-method` | RENAME | `goroutine-launch` | The "concrete-method" suffix conflates two orthogonal facts (concurrency edge vs. dispatch kind). - Codex's edge labels are slightly *too* descriptive: they encode receiver provenance (struct-field, channel-receive) into the dispatch label. For a general schema this will explode combinatorially. Receiver-source and dispatch-kind are better as orthogonal axes.
- Alternatives section is thorough and matches mine almost exactly.

## Cross-trace observations

All three traces converge on the same canonical minimal path: `main` → goroutine-launch into `(*Manager).Run` → channel-receive of `*pipe` and call to `(*pipe).NextSubscribers` → `(*pipe).newMessage` → `(*Manager).NewCampaignMessage`. The meaningful divergence is in edge-type granularity: codex over-specifies (encoding receiver provenance into dispatch labels, which won't generalize), gemini under-specifies (collapsing the channel-typed step into a generic concrete-method label and losing the one interesting static-analysis fact), and my own labels sit between — keeping `goroutine-launch` and `method-call-on-concrete-type` simple while breaking out `channel-typed-method-dispatch` because it is genuinely a composed type-flow + dispatch edge. Codex is closest to the canonical minimal path on path content; my labeling scheme is closest to the right granularity for a general schema.
