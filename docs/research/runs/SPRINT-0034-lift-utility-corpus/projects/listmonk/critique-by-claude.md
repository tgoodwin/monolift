## Overlooked

I did not identify any region that all three drafts collectively missed and that clearly belongs in the merged set. The strongest "near-miss" is the public archive render path (`cmd/archive.go:119`, `(*App).CampaignArchivePage`) — viral-newsletter spikes drive bursty load through `compileArchiveCampaigns` + `NewCampaignMessage` on a public unauthenticated endpoint — but it composes my C-1 and C-8 rather than introducing new compute, and it is in my own draft as C-10. The remaining bounce-webhook variants (`Postmark.ProcessBounce` at `internal/bounce/webhooks/postmark.go`, `Forwardemail.ProcessBounce` at `internal/bounce/webhooks/forwardemail.go:49`, `Lettermint.ProcessBounce` at the same package) are structurally identical-shape siblings of SES and Sendgrid and nobody scored them individually, but they pass the rubric for the same reasons codex's C-9/C-10 do; whether they belong as separate candidates or fold into a single "bounce webhook processor" entry is an aggregation question for Phase 3, not a Phase 1 omission.

## Overall observations

Codex and gemini converge cleanly on the five strongest picks (per-recipient render, both messenger pushes, CSV import, image thumbnail) — the same five I rank highest. They diverge on the marginal tier: codex picks the same set of borderline candidates I did (POP scan, template compile, transactional render, Sendgrid as a separately-scored sibling of SES), while gemini substitutes narrower or weaker units (`ConvertContent` instead of `CompileTemplate`; `classifyBounce` instead of `POP.Scan`; `ExtractZIP` instead of any defensible alternative). The systematic difference is on rubric criterion (3) coherent unit and the disqualifier list: codex consistently picks the surrounding meaningful region, while gemini gravitates to the inner pure-functional leaf even when that leaf is too small to justify the lift overhead, and once accepts a candidate (C-8) that fails its own rubric scoring on state independence. Codex is also more careful with file:line citations — every citation I spot-checked is correct or correct ±1, while gemini has at least three citation drifts (`cmd/campaigns.go:213` → `:244`; `cmd/media.go:49` → `:99`; `pop.go:142` → `:175`).
