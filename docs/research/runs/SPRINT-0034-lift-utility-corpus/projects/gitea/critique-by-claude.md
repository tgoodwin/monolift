## Overall observations

The two foreign drafts converge cleanly on the four strongest candidates — webhook delivery, archive generation, mirror pull sync, and repository migration — and they jointly pull in valuable picks I missed: codex's PR mergeability check (C-3), RPM metadata rebuild (C-8), workflow detection (C-9); gemini's password hashing (C-8) and language stats (C-9). They diverge most on the request-path CPU regions: codex consistently picks the *outer* envelope (`GetDiffForRender`, full-file `MigrateRepository`) where gemini sometimes drills into a sub-leaf (`diffLineWithHighlight`, Bleve `addUpdate`) that is redundant with the outer pick or has weaker state-independence. On rubric rigour: codex is systematically more accurate on file:line citations and more disciplined about distinguishing producer/consumer (queue handler vs. the function it calls), while gemini's line numbers are wrong on at least 7 of 10 candidates and a couple of its caller cites point at unrelated functions — the *regions* it identifies are real, but the line-level evidence is loose enough to need correction before the merge.
