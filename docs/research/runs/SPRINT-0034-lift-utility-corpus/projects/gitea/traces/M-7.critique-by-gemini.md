## Cross-trace observations

The traces converge on the initial startup sequence (`main` -> `runWeb` -> `InitWebInstalled` -> `pull.Init`). They diverge significantly on how to handle the asynchronous jump at the region root: Claude treats reaching the closure definition as sufficient, Codex follows a full producer-consumer loop, and Gemini uses a single high-level dispatch edge. Codex's trace is the most precise regarding line numbers and provides the most comprehensive view of the subsystem's liveness.
