## Cross-trace observations

- **Convergence:** All three traces converge on the second half of the path (routers -> services -> module) with high precision on line numbers and edge types (`http-handler-registration` and `direct-function-call`).
- **Divergence:** The traces diverge significantly on the CLI dispatch (Step 1). Gemini follows the execution flow (`RunMainApp` -> `app.Run`), Claude follows the registration flow (`NewMainApp` -> `newWebCommand`), and Codex uses a direct jump.
- **Canonical path:** Gemini's trace is the closest to a canonical path as it strictly follows the call graph from the binary head through the execution wrappers, while Claude and Codex prioritize the data-flow registration sites which are "harder" but not strictly the control-flow edges.
