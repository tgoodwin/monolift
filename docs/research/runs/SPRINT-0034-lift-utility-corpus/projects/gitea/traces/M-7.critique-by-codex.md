## Cross-trace observations

Both traces converge on the same startup route through the default `web` command and on the closure call at `services/pull/check.go:488`. They diverge on the queue: Claude describes the queue mechanics accurately but places them outside the path, while Gemini collapses them into an unverifiable worker-pool step. Claude is closer to the canonical minimal path once its liveness section is promoted into the main path and the `mustInit` split is collapsed.
