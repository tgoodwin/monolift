### Summary

- **Path length:** 11 steps
- **Edge types encountered:** `cli-command-action-dispatch`, `direct-function-call`, `function-value-parameter-call`, `function-value-registration`, `http-subrouter-mount`, `http-handler-registration`, `queue-work-item-dispatch`, `function-value-in-struct-field`
