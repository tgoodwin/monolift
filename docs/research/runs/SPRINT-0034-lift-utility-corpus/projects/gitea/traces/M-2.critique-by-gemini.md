## Cross-trace observations

The traces converge on the boot-time initialization path (`Init` -> `graceful` -> `queue` -> `worker`), which is the canonical way the archiver service is activated. Claude's trace is slightly more minimal by focusing on the resolution mechanisms (interfaces and closures), while Codex's trace is more exhaustive regarding the internal data flow of the queue (channels and batching). Both traces correctly identify the complex chain of function values in struct fields that connect the archiver package to the generic queue modules.
