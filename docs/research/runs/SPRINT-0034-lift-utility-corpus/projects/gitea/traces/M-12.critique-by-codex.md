## Cross-trace observations

Both traces converge on the same real tail: `stats.Init -> populateRepoIndexer -> statsQueue -> handler -> indexer.Index -> (*DBIndexer).Index -> GetLanguageStats`. They diverge mainly in queue modeling: claude preserves the `safeHandler` function-field dispatch but hides setup in prose, while gemini compresses the queue into a single coarse callback edge. Claude's trace is closer to the canonical minimal path, provided the queue registration and runner launch are promoted to explicit edges or a well-defined queue summary.
