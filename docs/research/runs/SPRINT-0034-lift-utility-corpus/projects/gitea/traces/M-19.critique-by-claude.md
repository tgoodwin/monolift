### Other notes
- Codex is the only trace to point out `fn.Call(argsIn)` at `modules/web/handler.go:181`. ## Critique of gemini's trace

### Path
- **Correct:** yes — citations verify, including `cmd/main.go:157` for `RunMainApp`.
- **Minimal:** no — Step 1 (`main → RunMainApp`) is a trivial wrapper: `RunMainApp` just sets up signals and calls `app.Run`. A whole-program analyzer can collapse it. Gemini also compresses what codex spreads across Steps 4–5 (subrouter mount + nested `Group` closures) into a single direct call, which loses the closure-traversal observation that the framework's `Group(pattern, fn)` synchronously executes `fn` to discover children. Net: one wasted step at the top, one missing step in the middle.

### Edge types
| Step | gemini's label | Verdict | Suggested label (if different) | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `direct-function-call` | OK if kept; better to drop the step entirely | — | Trivial forward; collapse. |
| 2 | `struct-literal-field-assignment` | RENAME | `function-value-in-struct-field` | The label names the *write* (where the value was placed) instead of the *edge* the analyzer must resolve (a function value living in a struct field and dispatched by the framework). The dispatch is the hard part, not the assignment. |
| 5 | `direct-function-call` | PARTIAL | `direct-function-call` + `immediate-closure-callback` | The call to `CommonRoutes` is direct, but reaching `r.Put("/upload", ...)` from there requires walking three nested `Router.Group(pattern, func(){...})` closures (`api.go:130`, `:313`, `:323`, `:325`). Gemini elides this; codex captures it. |
| 6 | `http-handler-registration` | OK | — | — |

### Other notes
- Gemini's "Hardest edge" prose (Step 6) gestures at nested closures and routing DSL but the trace itself does not represent that work as a distinct edge — there's a mismatch between the narrative and the labeled path.
- No mention of the reflective `fn.Call` at the dispatch site.

## Cross-trace observations

All three traces agree on the spine: `main → runWeb → serveInstalled → NormalRoutes → CommonRoutes → r.Put("/upload", debian.UploadPackageFile) → UploadPackageFile → BuildSpecificRepositoryFiles`. They diverge mostly in granularity at the framework boundaries — codex is most thorough (separates conditional-mount and closure-traversal as their own steps, and uniquely flags the reflective dispatch in `modules/web/handler.go`); gemini is shortest in spirit but adds a wasted top step and mislabels the cli dispatch as a struct-literal *assignment* edge; mine sits in the middle and is closest to the canonical minimal path at 6 steps. Codex's `immediate-closure-callback` is a label worth keeping; codex's `conditional-subrouter-mount` is not.
